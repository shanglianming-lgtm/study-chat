The entrance door, hallway and additional room geometry are generated by this project.
The GLB files in `models/` come from the user-supplied StudyChat-Model-Assets package. Its bookcase, folio, decorative bindings, window, wall, desk, chair, day/dusk exterior and desktop player are used in the room. White_Dress_Avatar.glb is the local player. The chestnut fringe and long loose hair follow the original style and use editable Bézier guides and a packed strand texture in the Blender source. The first-person view hides the face, scalp and hair at eye level. The Blender export script in `scripts/export-player.py` captures the relaxed bind pose and weights the skirt hem to the legs for walking and sitting. Its base is the pixiv VRoid Project CC0 Base_Female. The Blender sources and assembly notes are in the separate asset folder.

### Rebuilding the refined avatar

Run from the repository root with Blender 4.3.2:

```sh
blender -b "书房建模/04-avatar/White_Dress_Avatar.blend" --python scripts/export-player.py -- public/assets/models/White_Dress_Avatar.glb
cp public/assets/models/White_Dress_Avatar.glb "书房建模/04-avatar/玩家角色.glb"
```

The original editable Blender file and its proportions are preserved. The export
pass bakes the authored chestnut/umber brow and lash colours into RGBA textures
(Blender's legacy colour-mix nodes are not portable), keeps the full smooth body
and hand mesh, subdivides the face once, retains more garment folds, relaxes the
rolled hem and exports opaque cotton with explicit roughness. Hair guides and
UVs are unchanged. The resulting model is approximately 115k triangles / 7.8 MB,
up from 57k / 4.8 MB. The page-hand is derived from that same skin and rig; its
three-joint curls carry matching normal morphs and are framed smaller in reading.
